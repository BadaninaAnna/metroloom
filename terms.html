2. A calculation tool, not advice

Metroloom applies the methods of JCGM 100:2008 (GUM), EA-4/02 M and ILAC G8 to the data and criteria you enter — components, distributions, sensitivity coefficients, degrees of freedom, tolerance limits and decision rule. It shows the result of those inputs. It does not give professional metrological, legal or quality-management advice.

Statements such as pass or conditional fail follow mechanically from your inputs and the decision rule you chose. The decision on conformity, the choice of the measurement model and the completeness of the budget are always your responsibility.
